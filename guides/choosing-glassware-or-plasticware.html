Glassware or plasticware: which should you choose?

Each material suits different jobs. A quick guide to picking the right one for your practical.

12 October 2026, 3 min read

When glass is better

Borosilicate glass handles heat and resists most chemicals, and it is clear and easy to read. Use it for heating, titration and measuring when accuracy matters. It can break, so handle it carefully and check for chips before use.

When plastic is better

Plastic is light and does not shatter, which makes it good for younger students, storage and field work. Most plastic is not meant for heating, and some plastics can be affected by solvents, so check the product label.
