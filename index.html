Can I order or pay for products on this website?

No. This website is for information only. To ask about an item, call us, message us on WhatsApp or visit the store.

Does the website show whether an item is in stock?

No. Stock changes through the day, so we do not publish availability or prices online. Call or message us to check.

Do you supply items for schools and colleges?

Yes. We list glassware, plasticware, rubberware, metal and porcelain ware, filter papers, meters, apparatus, models and charts for biology, chemistry and physics. Send your list on WhatsApp and we will tell you what we have.

Can I send a photo of my requirement list?

Yes. A photo or typed list on WhatsApp is the quickest way to ask about many items at once.

Do you take bulk or institutional orders?

Yes. Schools, colleges and laboratories can ask about larger quantities by phone, WhatsApp or email.
